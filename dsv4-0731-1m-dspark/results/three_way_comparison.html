08

Verdict

Native 1M wins overall (78.7 vs 77.1/76.0) with 2.4× decode speed and 2× the context — but it takes both Sparks and 156 GB. With reasoning ON for all three, native dominates evalplus decisively (+8.5 HE+, +5.1 MBPP+ over v9a). v9a remains the champion single-box quant: tops Hard86, within 8–9pp of native on the hard evalplus suites at −65 GB and on one machine. v3 is the quality ceiling for quants (+2–4pp evalplus over v9a) at +12 GB. Serving rule of thumb: coding/agent serving on one Spark → v9a; long-context or max throughput with 2 Sparks available → native 1M.
