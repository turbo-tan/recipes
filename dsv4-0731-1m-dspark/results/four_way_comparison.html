06

Verdict

Native 1M (2×Spark) wins evalplus + throughput outright. R2_TQ3_4S on a single 3090 @1M is the standout: 91 GB, full 1M context from one consumer GPU at 14 tok/s, and it beats its own Spark deployment on every evalplus metric and Hard86 — proof the quant's quality is substrate-independent. On one Spark, R2 + drafter is the smallest, fastest, longest-context serving config (22.9 t/s @1M vs TQ3_4S's 21.4 @512K). Serving guide: max speed + 2 Sparks → native 1M; 1M on one consumer GPU → R2 3090; best single-Spark → R2 + drafter; extraction/instruction generalist → TQ3_4S.
