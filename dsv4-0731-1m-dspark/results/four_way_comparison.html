This config serves 1,048,576 context at 14.2 tok/s with arithmetic gate clean. --fit on guards against CUDA OOM by auto-sizing unset args to VRAM; -np 1 serializes the slot (avoids the queue-pileup crash seen with parallel-4 client + single slot). Hard86 77/86, HE+ 89.0, MBPP+ 77.5 all measured under exactly this config.
07

Verdict

Native 1M (2×Spark) wins evalplus + throughput outright. v9a on a single 3090 @1M is the standout: full 1M context from one consumer GPU at 14 tok/s, and it beats its own Spark deployment on every evalplus metric and Hard86 — proof the quant's quality is substrate-independent. v9a Spark remains the fastest single-box v9a (18.9 t/s). v3 keeps the dataextract lead. Serving guide: max speed + 2 Sparks → native 1M; 1M on one consumer GPU → v9a 3090; fastest single-Spark v9a → v9a Spark; extraction/instruction generalist → v3.
